Privacy Notice

Last updated: 27 November 2026

In short: this website does not ask for, collect or store any patient information. The queue only shows ticket numbers, never names or health details.

Who we are

Miranda Clinics (Dr. Kenneth Miranda and Dr. Kath Miranda), Bella Vista Subdivision, Brgy. Santiago, General Trias, Cavite. We are the personal information controller for this website under the Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173) and its implementing rules.

Patients and visitors

  • No forms, no accounts. The website has no sign-up, booking or contact form for patients, so you never give us personal information through it.
  • The queue shows numbers only. Nothing on the TV or the queue page links a number to a person, a visit or a health condition.
  • No tracking. We use no advertising or analytics cookies and no tracking tools. Visitors receive no cookies at all.
  • Technical requests. Like every website, our server briefly sees your device's internet (IP) address when your browser loads a page. We use it only to deliver the page and to block repeated wrong logins; it is held in memory for at most 15 minutes and never saved.
  • Fonts. The site's typeface is loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser also contacts Google when a page opens. See Google's privacy policy.

For appointments, results or anything about your health, please contact the clinic directly by phone or in person. Do not send medical details through the website.

Clinic staff and doctors who log in

To run the queue, the clinic gives each doctor and staff member their own login. For these accounts we keep:

  • name and username;
  • the password, stored only in scrambled (hashed) form that nobody can read back;
  • which doctors or rooms the account handles;
  • a sign-in cookie that keeps you logged in for up to 12 hours and is removed when you log out;
  • an activity log of what each account did (for example "Room 1: number 012") and when, kept to the latest 1,000 entries.

We use this only to run the queue, keep accounts secure and see who made a change. Accounts are removed when someone leaves the clinic.

Who can see it

Only the clinic's administrators. We do not sell, share or transfer this information to anyone else. It is stored on the clinic's own server.

How we protect it

Passwords are hashed, logins are limited after repeated wrong attempts, each person sees only what their role allows, and the site is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection.

Your rights

Under the Data Privacy Act you have the right to be informed, to access, to correct, to object, to have your data erased or blocked, to data portability, and to claim damages. To use any of these rights, contact us below. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may file a complaint with the National Privacy Commission.
